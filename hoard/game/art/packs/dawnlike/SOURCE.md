Crypt and graveyard props (coffins, tombstones, cobwebs) for the mourning corner and the debt-boss lair, ritual rugs under the shrine, a throne for the leader's seat, hooded cultists and robed humanoids for the chanting crowd at the back of the casino (2-frame idle), critters and fishing catches.
