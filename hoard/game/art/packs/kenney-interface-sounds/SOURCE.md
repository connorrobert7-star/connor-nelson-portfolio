## What Hoard uses it for

Case reel ticks (tick_001/002 once per item past the marker, rate and pitch following the uneven slowdown), glass_00x for the near-miss crawl past gold, click/pluck on every button squash, scratch_001-005 looped at finger speed for scratch-offs, glitch for the room going wrong when a streak breaks, bong for the dealer's card-down beat.
