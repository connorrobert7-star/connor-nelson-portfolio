# Google Fonts pixel set: Jacquard 12, Jacquarda Bastarda 9, Jersey 10, Micro 5, Pixelify Sans, Silkscreen, Tiny5

- **Author:** Sarah Cadigan-Fried / Soft Type; Stefie Justprince; Jason Kottke; Stefan Schmidt
- **Page:** https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Jacquard+12
- **License:** OFL-1.1
- **License evidence:** OFL.txt in each font folder (from github.com/google/fonts/ofl/<name>/OFL.txt): "This Font Software is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1." OFL allows bundling the fonts inside a game; they can't be sold on their own.
- **Status:** in repo, 0.96 MB (8 txt, 8 ttf)
- **Format:** TTF

## What Hoard uses it for

Jacquard 12 for cult titles over doors and on cutscene title cards; Jacquarda Bastarda 9 for shrine inscriptions; Jersey 10 for machine price tags and the odds on the wall ("1 IN 400"); Silkscreen caps labels; Tiny5 / Micro 5 for 5px numbers on follower nametags.

## Credit line (game credits)

Jacquard 12, Jacquarda Bastarda 9, Jersey 10, Micro 5 by Sarah Cadigan-Fried; Pixelify Sans by Stefie Justprince; Silkscreen by Jason Kottke; Tiny5 by Stefan Schmidt (SIL OFL 1.1)

## What was kept and changed

Variable-font files renamed: 'Tiny5[BLED,JITT,ROND,wdth,wght].ttf' to Tiny5-Variable.ttf and 'PixelifySans[wght].ttf' to PixelifySans-Variable.ttf (brackets break res:// paths). OFL is not CC but is free and allows redistribution with the game.

Recolor to the master palette in docs/ASSETS.md before use (rarity colors only where rarity is meant).
