## What Hoard uses it for

Shell of every dark room: casino walls and floor, crypt, gate, shrine and the three dungeon rooms (dark_kosinaz floor as the dungeon base). Wall banners recolored to the five rarity colors hang over each machine as the odds marker. Wall fountains run as the coin fountain when a deposit lands. Chest open animations (full, empty, mimic) for case reveals. Extended red runner carpet (recolored oxblood) is the aisle to the dealer; extended candle and torch flames light other packs' candles. Dungeon fodder and room bosses (imp, chort, goblin, big_demon, ogre). pumpkin_dude is a seasonal follower skin.
