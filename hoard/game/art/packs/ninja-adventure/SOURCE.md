## What Hoard uses it for

Backbone of the cast. Actor/Character: robed Monk, Monk2, Shaman and Villager chibis are the Cult of the Lamb flock; Actor/Animal cats, dogs, frogs, racoons, hamsters and pigs are Webkinz-style pets; Actor/CharacterAnimated/NinjaGreen (32x32, Net and Pickaxe overlays) is the leader template for fishing and the mine; Actor/Boss/GiantSlime is the debt boss (GiantSlime2 for the interest-heal phase); Spirit and GiantSpirit ghosts for mourning; 38x38 facesets as dealer close-up stand-ins. Ui/Emote mood bubbles, Ui/Receptacle fill orbs for mood and hunger and fund jars, Ui/Dialog for the dealer's word bubble, Items/Food for follower food, Items/Treasure coin spin for the fountain. FX: spark-circle rarity flash, rings, smoke, Raylight shafts and Fog for the casino haze. Backgrounds: village house exteriors, idol statues, torii gate, stalls, the fishing pond, FishNet. Audio: Voice1-10 follower babble, Coin/Gold, Success/Secret/GameOver jingles, Water and Bubble for fishing, Hit/Impact for the boss; music for the village (Lost Village), the gate (Curse), the dealer (Tension), the casino after hours (Dark Castle), fishing (Aquatic) and mourning (Sad Theme).
