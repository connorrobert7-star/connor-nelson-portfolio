## What Hoard uses it for

The real-money sound and only for real money: Coins_Pouring scaled by the dollar amount under the coin fountain, Coins_Several per particle wave, Coins_Single per floating number, Gems_Single when a cosmetic slots into the collection book. Coins earned by play use the 8-bit coins instead.
