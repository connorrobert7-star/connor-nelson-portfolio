Machine faces (9-slice frames), the dealer's odds placard (headers and banners), the case-reel window with arrow markers as the needle, skull-arched portrait frames for followers and collection cells, the bony-hand cursor, 32x32 icon buttons with real pressed states, bars for the debt boss HP and the pity floor.
