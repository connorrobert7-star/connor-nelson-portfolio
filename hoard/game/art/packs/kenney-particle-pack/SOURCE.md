## What Hoard uses it for

Soft white masks tinted per use: PointLight2D textures under glowing machines and shrine candles (circle, light), additive glints on reveals (star, flare), a faint second layer under the summoning circle (magic), the bloom halo behind legendary items, smoke puffs.

## What was kept and changed

47 of 80 textures kept and downscaled from 512x512 to 128x128 (Lanczos) for size; they are soft glows, so the loss is invisible at 480x270.

Recolor to the master palette in docs/ASSETS.md before use (rarity colors only where rarity is meant).
