## What Hoard uses it for

Base body for the leader and followers at 32x32: Character-Base is a bare 8-direction body, so won cosmetics (robes, masks, hats, halos per tier) are drawn as aligned overlay sheets. Human-Worker for villagers; PunyMonsters cat, dog and rat as pets that walk beside the leader; hurt and death rows for the dungeon and the mourning collapse.
